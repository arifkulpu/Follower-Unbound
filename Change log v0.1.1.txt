# Follower Unbound - Changelog v0.1.1

## Overview
This update focuses on making follower needs consumption reliable, adding combat potion usage with configurable thresholds, supporting modded waterskins/drinks (SunHelm), and polishing companion behavior after sleeping and waiting.

---

## What's New & Improvements

### 1. Reliable Autonomous Eating & Drinking Loop
- **Background Active Loop:** Added a periodic background tick that checks follower needs and inventory every second. Companions no longer get stuck waiting for a menu or event to trigger their meals.
- **Gradual Consumption (Hysteresis Sessions):** Followers start eating or drinking once their need drops below the configured threshold (default 50%) and continue until they are full (100%), taking natural pauses between items.

### 2. Delay After Sleeping & Waiting
- Followers will no longer immediately eat or drink right after waking up or finishing a wait session.
- A built-in **5-second cooldown** ensures companions transition naturally after sleep/wait before consuming items.

### 3. Missing Food & Drink HUD Alerts
- If a follower is hungry or thirsty but lacks food or drinks in their inventory, a notification appears in the top-left corner of your screen (e.g., "Goblina is thirsty, but has nothing to drink!").
- An alert cooldown (60 seconds per follower) prevents notification spam.

### 4. Need Priority Ordering
- Follower processing now prioritizes the companion with the greatest need first (the one with the lowest hunger or thirst score acts before others).

### 5. Full SunHelm Waterskin & Custom Drink Compatibility
- Added full support for SunHelm waterskins (1-use, 2-use, 3-use waterskins including FormID 714DE9B0) and modded water bottles.
- Followers can now detect and drink from waterskins and water bottles to quench their thirst.

### 6. In-Combat Potion Usage & Configurable Thresholds
- **Dynamic Combat Potions:** During combat, followers automatically drink Health, Magicka, or Stamina potions when their stats drop below configured percentages.
- **Inventory Removal & Cooldowns:** Potions are consumed, animated, and properly removed from follower inventories, with safety cooldowns to prevent spam.
- **Customizable Thresholds:** Added configurable sliders in SKSE Menu Framework / INI settings:
  - Health Potion Threshold (default: 40%)
  - Magicka Potion Threshold (default: 30%)
  - Stamina Potion Threshold (default: 30%)

### 7. Yapılan Akıllı Düzenleme: iksir kullanımı iyikeştirmesi:
İhtiyaç Açığına (Aciliyet Puanına) Göre Öncelik:

Sabit "Can > Magicka > Stamina" sırası kaldırıldı. Artık takipçinin o an hangi değeri eşiğe göre daha çok düşmüşse (yani hangisi daha acilse) ilk o iksiri içmeyi dener.
Büyücü sınıflarında (Mage, Necromancer vb.) büyü atamaz hale gelmemeleri için Magicka ihtiyacına ek aciliyet bonusu verildi. Savaşçılarda da Stamina açığı gözetildi.
Ardışık Aynı İksir Önleme (Rotasyon):

Takipçi az önce Can İksiri içtiyse ve canı kritik/ölümcül (%20 altı) değilse, sistem doğrudan yine Can İksiri içmek yerine sıradaki tükenmiş ihtiyaca (Magicka veya Stamina) şans verir.
Böylece büyücüler bir tur can iksiri içtikten sonra büyüleri bittiğinde sonraki turda manasız kalmayıp hemen Magicka iksirini içerler.
Acil Hayatta Kalma Koruması (%20 Altı):

Eğer takipçinin canı %20 veya altına inmişse, hayatta kalma her şeyden önce gelir ve Can İksiri mutlak birinciliği alır.

## Bug Fixes & Stability
- **Fixed Eating Freeze After Sleep:** Resolved an issue where the 5-second sleep cooldown permanently prevented eating because there was no recurring check to resume consumption after the timer expired.
- **Dialogue Safety Check:** Follower consumption animations and messages are paused while talking with NPCs to avoid animation cuts or UI conflicts.
- **Inventory Trading Cooldown:** A short 3-second delay is applied after closing container or barter menus before companions resume eating.
